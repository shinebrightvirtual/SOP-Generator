2 — Why It Matters

Before we had this process, new clients would fall through the cracks — welcome emails would go out late, folders wouldn't get set up, and sometimes clients would show up to their first call without any of the prep materials. It felt chaotic and unprofessional for a studio that prides itself on client experience.

Every new client gets a warm, organised welcome within 24 hours of signing. Their folders are ready, their first call is booked, and they feel genuinely taken care of from day one.

Clients notice when things feel rushed or disjointed right at the start. First impressions set the tone for the whole working relationship — if onboarding feels scattered, it shakes their confidence in us before the work has even begun.

3 — When & How It Runs

A new client signs their contract and pays their deposit. We get an automated notification from HoneyBook when both are complete.

The client has received their welcome email, their project folder is fully set up in Google Drive, their intake form is submitted, and their first call is confirmed on the calendar.

The proposal and contract need to be fully signed and the deposit collected before we start. We also need their contact details and project brief in HoneyBook.

Project kick-off can't happen until onboarding is done. The project management setup in ClickUp and the first deliverable timeline both depend on the intake form being completed.

4 — The Overview

  1. Confirm contract and deposit
  2. Set up client folder in Google Drive
  3. Send welcome email with intake form
  4. Set up project in ClickUp
  5. Book kick-off call
  6. Run kick-off call

5 — Step by Step

Step 1

Check HoneyBook for the signed contract and deposit confirmation. If both are marked complete, move the lead to the 'Active Client' pipeline stage.

Tools: HoneyBook Time: 5 min
Step 2

Duplicate the 'Client Template' folder in Google Drive and rename it with the client name and project year. Move it into the correct service folder. Share with the client and give them comment access to the shared deliverables subfolder only.

Tools: Google Drive Time: 10 min
Step 3

Send the welcome email using the template in HoneyBook. Personalise the first two lines with the client's name and what they've booked. Attach the intake form link. BCC the studio inbox.

Tools: HoneyBook, Gmail Time: 10 min
Step 4

Create a new project in ClickUp using the onboarding template for the relevant service. Set the start date to today and the target completion date based on the project scope. Assign yourself and any team members.

Tools: ClickUp Time: 10 min
Step 5

Send the Calendly scheduling link to the client via email so they can pick their kick-off call time. Once they book, add any prep notes to the calendar event.

Tools: Calendly, Google Calendar Time: 5 min
Step 6

Run the kick-off call using the call guide doc in the client's Google Drive folder. Take notes directly in the doc. Record the call if the client has consented. Send a follow-up summary within 2 hours.

Tools: Zoom, Google Docs Time: 60 min
